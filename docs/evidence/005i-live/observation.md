TOKYO - U.S. President Donald Trump has briefed Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi "in detail" about his trilateral talks on Thursday with the Chinese and Russian leaders, while reaffirming his commitment to the Japan-U.S. alliance and the Indo-Pacific region.

The Japanese Foreign Ministry said Friday that Takaichi and Trump agreed during their 20-minute phone conversation to continue promoting a free and open Indo-Pacific, following a recent Quad group summit with Australia and India. [...] Following her phone talks with Trump, Takaichi told reporters on Thursday that Japan will work with the United States and like-minded partners to uphold a free and open international order.

"We will elevate relations between Japan and the United States, now more trusted allies than ever, to even greater heights," she said, adding she exchanged views with Trump on the situation in Ukraine.

Trump also briefed Takaichi on his bilateral talks with Xi that took place in Shenzhen. [...] Takaichi and Trump attended Association of Southeast Asian Nations-related meetings in Manila, where the Quad summit was held, and the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum summit in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen.
